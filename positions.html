We are looking for systems neuroscientists who want to design and run experiments, and analyze the data they collect. Our behavior, recording and analysis pipelines are already up and running, so new trainees can spend their time designing and carrying out projects.

Typical projects

You will use some combination of:

  • Two-photon calcium imaging of 1,000–100,000 neurons simultaneously in mouse cortex or hippocampus, often across multiple days in the same animals.
  • Behavioral training, including virtual-reality paradigms and visual learning tasks.
  • Two-photon optogenetics, to perturb specific populations while recording.
  • Analysis of the resulting datasets, in Python, using a range of models and approaches.

You would own a scientific question and see it through from experimental design to publication. See here for some examples: unsupervised learning, predictive coding, behavioral drive of cortical activity.

Requirements

Essential

  • Hands-on experience with in vivo experiments in animals, such as imaging, electrophysiology or optogenetics.
  • Experience analyzing your own neural or behavioral data in Python, including basic statistics and visualization.
  • Familiarity with standard data-analysis and modeling methods, such as dimensionality reduction, regression and neural-network models.
  • Genuine curiosity about how populations of neurons compute and learn.

Not required

  • A formal background in machine learning, statistics or computer science.
  • Experience building software: the lab maintains the pipelines.
  • Prior work at this scale.

Lab resources and strengths

  • Recording infrastructure at a large scale, including microscopes and protocols developed in-house for imaging tens of thousands of neurons.
  • Analysis pipelines that already work. Suite2p, Kilosort, Cellpose, Facemap and Rastermap are developed and maintained by the PIs and by dedicated software engineers in the lab.
  • Close mentorship from both PIs, who co-supervise most projects, giving you two advisors rather than one.
  • A strong open-science record. We release the data and analysis code from every study, which makes lab work visible and citable well beyond the paper.
  • A track record of placing people. Recent postdocs have gone on to faculty positions.
  • Full funding from the Howard Hughes Medical Institute, with competitive salary and benefits, and support for conference travel.

Postdocs

Please email Carsen Stringer (stringerc@hhmi.org) with:

  • your CV.
  • a short description of what you have worked on and what you would like to work on next.
  • contact details for three references.

For information about Janelia, please visit www.janelia.org/about-us. For more information about being at Janelia, please check out these videos. Janelia provides incredible support for scientific research (surgical support, animal behavior training, and optical engineering), enabling researchers to focus on their specific scientific questions.

Graduate students

We are not currently taking graduate students, but let us know if you are applying in Fall 2027.

Research technicians

Positions for research technicians open periodically, for people who are finishing their undergraduate/master’s degree or recently graduated (we do not mentor high school students in the lab). Recent technicians have gone on to PhD programs at JHU, Princeton, Columbia, and Mila. Email us if you are interested, even if nothing is posted.

Working here

We care about running a lab that is rigorous and also exciting to be in. We expect everyone in the group to be generous with their time, to share data and code openly, and to treat colleagues with kindness, both inside the lab and across the wider scientific community. Applicants should be committed to the same.
